# Product economics and smaller active catalogue

Each order card and product detail shows tax-exclusive unit selling price, gross margin, expected monthly sales, gross profit and sold units. Gross margin=(selected selling price - purchase cost)/selected selling price; selling price follows discount/premium except fixed-price rods/reels. Forecast uses same current-state engine, current quantities, action, season, delayed attraction, capacity and stock, with noise=1. Does not mutate state. Invalid plan/closed month displays unavailable sales. Profit excludes wages, rent and disposal; explanatory copy distinguishes sales from cash receipts.

Wear removed from all active inventory/order/assortment views and cannot unlock in year two. The internal eighth index is retained solely for old-save compatibility and historical journals. On the next month close, old wear stock is returned at cost plus applicable VAT, with explicit stock/VAT journal entries, no profit, and visible migration notice. This is a one-off version-change return rule, not a general supplier return policy. No silent balance deletion or index shift. Obsolescence ignores retired stock until return.

Verification: 35 engine/accounting regression cases (including deterministic product forecast equality, pricing/margin, retired inventory return and cash bridge) plus DOM smoke. New counts are five initially available categories plus frozen/live bait when equipped. Catalogue reduction makes full assortment easier; deterministic ordinary-policy years now -1,447,740 / -34,783 / +384,470 yen. Prior exact scenario figures are superseded; no automatic compensating cost penalties added.
