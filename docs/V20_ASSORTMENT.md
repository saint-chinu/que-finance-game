# Assortment protection

Eligible categories are those currently unlocked by year/equipment. After monthly sales and waste, stocked eligible categories / total eligible categories determines protection. Natural attraction loss, including excess-attraction decay, is multiplied by (1 - stocked ratio). Full assortment means zero natural decay; pending marketing gains still mature. Locked categories are excluded until unlocked. This supersedes the earlier rule that every no-marketing month strictly loses attraction: maintaining a full assortment is now a valid retention strategy, with inventory cash and obsolescence costs.

Displays current ratio in store overview and month-end ratio/actual decay in results. Investment simulations use the same rule. Category-specific thresholds and sale-out tests cover partial assortment, zero stock, full stock, equipment unlock and year unlock.

Noise=1 ordinary recommended replenishment with November/February improvements now yields year 1 -1,447,740, year 2 -215,230, year 3 +110,923 yen. These replace earlier balance figures; no unrelated cost increases were added to cancel the user's assortment benefit.
